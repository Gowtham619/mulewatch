# Arcadia Bank - Cyber-Fraud Complaint, Lien and Debit Freeze SOP
doc_id: SOP-02
doc_type: SOP

> Synthetic internal document created for the MuleWatch demo. Not legal advice.

## Trigger
This SOP applies when the bank receives a cyber-fraud complaint, or a request from a law-enforcement agency, naming one of its accounts as a beneficiary of defrauded funds - for example via the national cyber-crime reporting portal and helpline 1930.

## Golden hour
Speed determines recovery. On receipt, the fraud operations desk must, within the service window, trace the funds from the named beneficiary account to onward accounts within the bank and mark a lien for the disputed amount on available balances.

## Freeze and lien
Debit freeze on the full account is permitted only where the complaint, law-enforcement request or investigation provides grounds; otherwise mark a lien limited to the disputed amount. Record the legal basis and the officer approving the action.

## Linked accounts
If traced funds have moved to other internal accounts, the same steps apply to those accounts. The FCU must open a network case when three or more internal accounts are linked by transfers or shared devices.

## Customer communication
Use standard operational wording. Do not reveal the existence of an investigation or STR (see POL-01, tipping off).
