# Arcadia Bank - Customer Due Diligence, EDD and Re-KYC Triggers

## Risk-based approach
Customers are risk-categorised at onboarding and periodically. Video-based KYC and digital onboarding are permitted with safeguards, including liveness checks and verification that the customer is not being prompted by a third party.

## Enhanced due diligence triggers
Apply EDD when: account activity is materially inconsistent with declared occupation or income; the customer appears in adverse media or on internal or law-enforcement watchlists; the account is linked to a mule network or to cyber-fraud complaints; or KYC notes record red flags such as third-party presence, an unnamed referring agent, or inability to explain the purpose of the account.

## EDD actions
Obtain source-of-funds documents, verify employment or business, review device and login history, and consider restricting high-risk channels pending review. Escalate to the FCU if suspicion persists.

## Adverse media screening
Name matches must be confirmed using additional identifiers (city, age, occupation) before being treated as relevant. Namesakes must be documented and discarded.
