## Definition
A money mule account is an account used to receive and move proceeds of crime on behalf of others, typically cyber-enabled fraud such as investment scams, task-based job scams, digital arrest scams and phishing. Mule account holders may be complicit, paid for "renting" their account, or unwitting. Aged accounts are increasingly bought or rented to avoid new-account controls.

## Behavioural red flags
The following indicators, alone or in combination, should raise suspicion of mule activity:
1. Rapid pass-through: most credited funds leave the account the same day, often within minutes or a few hours.
2. Fan-in from many unrelated remitters, especially first-time remitters spread across several states.
3. Turnover materially inconsistent with the declared occupation and income, for example students, homemakers or gig workers receiving several lakh rupees a month.
4. New accounts (under 90 days) receiving high value soon after opening, or immediate requests to raise UPI/IMPS limits.
5. Cash-out patterns: repeated ATM withdrawals at or near the daily limit, withdrawals in multiple cities, or transfers to virtual digital asset (crypto) exchanges.
6. Several accounts operated from the same mobile device, SIM or IP address, or devices showing rooting/emulation.
7. The account is named as beneficiary in cyber-fraud complaints received through the national cyber-crime reporting portal or helpline 1930.
8. Vague narration on high-value transfers such as "personal", "help", "loan return" or "family support".

## Network view
Mule activity is organised. When one mule account is identified, investigators must examine linked accounts: counterparties that received onward transfers (layer accounts), accounts sharing devices, and accounts that withdraw the funds (cash-out accounts). Reporting the network as one case, with each account's role, is preferred to separate single-account reports.

## Response
High-confidence mule networks must be escalated to the Principal Officer's team the same day. Where complaints are received, the Cyber-Fraud SOP (SOP-02) governs lien marking and debit freeze. An STR is expected for confirmed mule networks.
