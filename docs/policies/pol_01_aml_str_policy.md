## Purpose and scope
This policy sets out how the bank identifies, escalates and reports suspicious transactions under its obligations as a reporting entity under the Prevention of Money Laundering Act framework. It applies to all deposit, wallet and payment accounts, all channels (branch, UPI, IMPS, NEFT, RTGS, ATM, cash) and all customer segments.

## What makes a transaction suspicious
A transaction, or an attempted transaction, is suspicious when it gives rise to a reasonable ground of suspicion that it may involve proceeds of crime, appears to be made in circumstances of unusual or unjustified complexity, appears to have no economic rationale or bona fide purpose, or may be linked to terrorist financing. Suspicion is assessed on the totality of facts: customer profile, transaction pattern, counterparties, channel and external information such as complaints or law-enforcement requests.

The value of a transaction is not, by itself, a reason to report or not to report. Attempted transactions that are abandoned must also be considered.

## Reporting timeline
Once the Principal Officer's team concludes that a transaction or series of transactions is suspicious, the Suspicious Transaction Report (STR) must be furnished to FIU-IND without delay and in any case within seven working days of arriving at that conclusion. The date of conclusion must be recorded in the case file. Investigations should not be kept open without progress to avoid the timeline.

## Tipping off and confidentiality
Staff must not disclose to the customer or any third party that an STR has been or may be filed. Account restrictions applied while an investigation is in progress must be communicated using standard operational language only.

## Roles
The Financial Crime Unit (FCU) investigates alerts and drafts STRs. The Principal Officer, or a delegate in the Principal Officer's team, reviews and approves each STR. Automated or AI-generated drafts are permitted only as decision support; a named human reviewer must approve every filing and remains accountable for its content.

## Record keeping
Case files, evidence, drafts, approvals and the final STR must be retained for at least five years from the date of the report, in a form that allows reconstruction of the decision.
